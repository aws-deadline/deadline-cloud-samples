## Company
- **Name:** Saffron Stack
- **Founded:** 2016 in Brooklyn, NY
- **HQ:** Brooklyn, NY (1132 Bedford Avenue)
- **Concept:** Vegetarian Chipotle-style Indian fast-casual
- **Tagline:** "Layered with love"
- **Logo/mascot:** stylized marigold flower
- **Locations:** 87 as of 2026 (mostly East Coast + Chicago, Austin, LA)
- **Status:** IPO rumored for 2027

## Founders & key people
- **Priya Iyer** — Co-founder & CEO. Chef formerly at Junoon NYC.
- **Arun Iyer** — Co-founder & COO. Priya's older brother. Wharton MBA, ex-Bain consultant.
- **Chef Vikram Mehta** — Culinary Director. Ex-Junoon, joined 2018.

## Signature build templates ("preset bowls")
- **The Bombay Bowl** — jeera rice + chana masala + paneer + mint chutney + lachha pyaaz
- **The Kathmandu Bowl** — jhol momos over chow chow noodles + achaar
- **The Hakka Bowl** — hakka noodles + chilli paneer + schezwan + crispy onions
- **The Punjab Spread** — dal makhani + palak paneer + butter naan + jeera rice
- **The Coastal Special** — coconut rice + South Indian sambar + spinach-cheese momos
- **The Dilli Special** — rotating monthly bowl
- **Little Tarka** — kids' line, smaller portions

## Programs & jargon
- **The Stack** — staff name for the assembly-line build process
- **Layer Up** — the loyalty program name
- **Stack Pass** — loyalty card
- **Golden Status** — top loyalty tier, earned after 75 visits; perks include free Saffron Stack on Diwali every year
- **The Tarka** — 5-day cook training program at Brooklyn HQ, every new kitchen hire completes it
- **Quick Saffron** — drive-thru / app-order channel; about 40% of orders

## Timeline / key events
- **2016** — First location opens at 1132 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn
- **2018** — Featured in Eater NY's "10 New Brooklyn Restaurants You Need to Try"; Chef Vikram joins
- **2019** — Crossed 10 locations; expanded to Manhattan
- **2020** — Pandemic pivot: launched Tiffin Tuesdays subscription meal program
- **2021** — Series A: $25M from Coral Tree Ventures
- **2022** — Launched Saffron Grain base; James Beard semifinalist nomination
- **2023** — Hit 50 locations; expanded to Chicago, Austin, LA
- **2024** — Launched **Stack at Home**, a self-service catering program for parties / weddings / corporate events ordered through the Quick Saffron app
- **2025** — Launched **Heritage Bowls** series: rotating monthly bowls highlighting regional Indian cuisines (Hyderabadi, Bengali, Goan, Gujarati, Kashmiri, etc.). Each month Chef Vikram collaborates with a guest chef from that region.
- **2026** — 87 locations; IPO rumored

## Quirky / brand details
- Every store has a real marigold growing in the front window
- The Brooklyn flagship has a wall of polaroids of customers' first visits
- Bowl lids printed with "stack jokes" (food puns)
- Layer Up members get a free Saffron Stack on Diwali every year
- Diwali is the busiest day of the year
- Brand colors: saffron orange + marigold yellow + deep green
- Music in stores rotates between Bollywood, Indian classical, and Indie
